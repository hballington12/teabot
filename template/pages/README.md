# pages

Your teabot's own pages, which its site shows when they are here:

- `privacy.md`: your privacy policy, at `/privacy`. Say what your teabot keeps about members and servers, for how long, and how to have it deleted.
- `terms.md`: your terms of service, at `/terms`.

They are Markdown. Raw HTML in them shows as text. The site links to each from its footer once the file exists. This README is not shown.
