# teabot

teabot is an open-source, plugin-based Discord bot. You run its commands in Discord, connect AI assistants to it, and choose the plugins your server needs.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Using teabot**

    ---

    For server admins: add teabot to your server, turn on plugins, choose who can use what, and connect an AI assistant. No programming needed.

    [Add teabot to your server](use/index.md)

-   **Self-hosting**

    ---

    For running your own teabot, with Docker, in about 15 minutes. Then keep it running, backed up and up to date.

    [Quick start](host/index.md)

-   **Writing plugins**

    ---

    For Python developers: add your own commands, with their own tables, jobs and tests, in a few files.

    [Your first plugin](plugins/index.md)

</div>
