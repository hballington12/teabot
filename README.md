<p align="center">Teabot is a scalable, open-source, plugin-based bot for Discord. People use it through slash commands, LLMs through MCP, and your own services through HTTP routes.</p>

## Why Teabot?

- **Easy to use.** Connect it to any MCP client, such as Claude Code, in a few minutes, and let it handle your Discord management the modern way.
- **Freely customisable.** Make Teabot work for you. Pick the plugins your server needs, and leave paywalled bot features behind.
- **Endless possibilities.** The plugin SDK wires your own plugins straight into Teabot, so you skip the boring parts and get straight to making your server what you want it to be.
- **Safe by design.** Set the permissions and Teabot enforces them. You choose who does what, and destructive changes always await confirmation.

## Quick start

1. **Add our bot.** A hosted Teabot is on its way, free for early adopters. Watch this repository to hear when it opens.
2. **Host it yourself.** Follow the [self-hosting guide](https://github.com/hballington12/teabot/blob/main/docs/self-hosting.md): with Docker, you have your own Teabot running in about half an hour.

## Plugins

| Plugin | What it does |
|---|---|
| [`teabot-announcements`](https://pypi.org/project/teabot-announcements/) | Posts an announcement to a channel, now or at a set time. |

A plugin hub for community plugins is coming. To write your own, see [Writing a plugin](https://github.com/hballington12/teabot/blob/main/docs/plugin-authoring.md).
