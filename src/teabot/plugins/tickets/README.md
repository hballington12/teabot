# tickets

- A first-party plugin: queues of items members claim, such as game accounts, and handlers who accept or reject the claims. What follows an accept belongs to whoever subscribes to its events.
- What it does, its commands and who can use them: https://teabot-dev.github.io/teabot/use/plugins/tickets/
- External connections: none.
- Tests: `tests/plugins/tickets/`, through `teabot.testing`.
